        What we check

        Six things scammers hope you miss.

        Can you sell?

        We test whether a coin can really be sold, to catch honeypots: coins you can buy but never sell. This test isn't available on Solana.

        Who's in control

        Hidden owners, ownership that can be taken back, freeze keys, and powers to pause or blacklist.

        Unlimited minting

        Whether the creator can print new coins and water down what you hold.

        Locked liquidity

        If the trading pool isn't locked, the creator can pull it and leave you stuck.

        Whale wallets

        How much the top wallets and the creator hold, and could dump at once.

        Fees & age

        Buy and sell taxes, fees that can be changed later, and how new the coin is.
